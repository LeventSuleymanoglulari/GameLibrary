## Direction contract

THESIS: The library is a shelf. The person pulls one case into the side pane. Filters, the shelf, and that pane share one window. Stacked sheets are the arrangement this surface refuses.

OWN-WORLD: Walnut ink and lamp amber, on a night desk and on morning paper. Spines are rows, not cards. Amber marks the pulled case, the rating, and the primary add action.

STORY: The person scans the shelf, filters without leaving, and edits status beside the list. Add occupies the same pane a case would, then the new case stays open.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A left rail carries the name and four filters. The center is the shelf of spines. The right pane is the empty invitation, the pulled case, or the add flow. Oyun Ekle is in the toolbar and fills the right pane.

FORM: Prototype Raf, shelf plus inspector, raised past the throwaway HTML. Pinned by the layout study. concept-seed was not rolled because that brief already chose the form. Signature motion is the right pane arriving with a short horizontal offset and a fade, 280ms. Reduce Motion keeps a 120ms fade.
